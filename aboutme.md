<p>Ave, munde, and thanks for stopping by. I’m Christopher Black, a senior solutions engineer working at the intersection of AI, security, software engineering, and sales. I help enterprise organizations modernize how they build, ship, and run software, reduce risk, and adopt platforms that materially improve how their engineering teams work.</p>

<p>I currently work as a Senior Solutions Engineer at Traversal, where we build AI that helps engineering teams find the root cause of production incidents. I partner with engineering and executive stakeholders to bring that into complex, real-world environments, aligning technical architecture with business outcomes and helping teams resolve incidents faster with greater confidence.</p>

<p>Previously, I was a Senior Solutions Engineer at Harness, working on CI/CD, infrastructure as code, and security automation. Before that, I worked at Lacework supporting cloud security and DevSecOps programs in highly regulated environments, and earlier in my career across several high-growth startups, including CircleCI. I’ve spent much of my professional life in the engine room, translating between builders and decision makers and operating where technical depth meets commercial reality.</p>

<p>I completed my Master’s degree in Cybersecurity at UC Berkeley. The program sharpened my understanding of modern threat models, distributed systems, and secure software development, reinforcing a belief I hold strongly: security and velocity are not opposing forces when designed correctly.</p>

<p>Beyond work, I volunteer with Larkin Street Youth Services, teaching computer science and career readiness to homeless and at-risk youth. It is the most meaningful work I do. Larkin Street supported me during a difficult chapter of my life, and giving back is both a responsibility and a privilege.</p>

<p>I am a student of systems: technical, historical, and human. I read Latin, study history, build infrastructure, and remain endlessly curious about how technology shapes institutions and society. Public speaking through Toastmasters has been a core discipline in developing clarity under pressure, a skill that translates directly to high-stakes enterprise conversations.</p>

<p>This site, <em>Aedifex</em> (Latin for “builder”), is my field journal: a place to write about AI, DevOps, security, platform strategy, and the realities of modern pre-sales engineering, with the occasional detour into Latin and history. If you care about building durable systems, aligning technology with revenue, and thinking a layer deeper about how software organizations operate, you’re in the right place.</p>
