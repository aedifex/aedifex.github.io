## The question

Rome conquered most of Britain and stopped at the sea. What, if anything, crossed it?

## What the ancient sources say

- Tacitus, *Agricola* 24: Agricola looks across at Ireland and claims it could be taken and held with a single legion and some auxiliaries.
- Ptolemy, *Geography* (2nd century): a map of Ireland with named coastal peoples, rivers and settlements. Where did that knowledge come from?
- Notes to add: Juvenal, later references to the *Scotti*.

## What the ground says

- Drumanagh, Co. Dublin: a promontory fort with Roman finds. Trading post, raid, or something more?
- Stonyford, Co. Kilkenny: a Roman-style burial.
- Coin hoards and trade goods along the east coast.
